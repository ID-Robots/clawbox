# Settings app

Every Settings section: Providers and the ClawBox AI pitch, Local AI, Harness tiles and the swap dialog, Voice, System, System Update, About. Read it when you touch `SettingsApp.tsx` or a panel it hosts.

## Frontend (`src/components/`)

### Built-in Apps

- **`SettingsApp.tsx`** — Providers, Local AI, Harness (the harness picker and the "Working on its own" background-jobs switches — `BackgroundJobsPanel.tsx` over `src/lib/background-jobs.ts`, the harness's own keys in both directions; ON by default since the owner's ruling of 2026-09-15, which reversed TASK-609's opt-outs — moved out of System on 2026-09-07 because their switches pushed the device's figures off the screen; `HarnessPicker.tsx`'s strings are `settings.harness*` keys in all ten locales now, not English literals; since 2026-09-15 `ClawboxMcpPanel.tsx` sits between the picker and the background jobs — the on/off switch for the assistant's device tools, writing `harness/mcp`, a "Restarting the assistant…" note while the write is in flight and a refusal in the route's own words, `settings.mcp.*` keys in all ten locales, test ids `clawbox-mcp-panel` / `clawbox-mcp-switch`), Channels (Telegram, Discord, Email), Voice, Network, Remote Control, Appearance, System, System Update, About (Coding Agent settings live in the Coding Agent app itself). On Harness a LOCKED box — every shipped SKU — draws BOTH harnesses as tiles (`/openclaw-logo.svg`, `/hermes-agent.png`, a tagline each, the health dot and a THIS BOX chip on the one the box runs) and on the other **Switch to <name>** (`swap_horiz`), with a plan row under them read from `GET /setup-api/harness/swap` (the ClawBox AI plan through its `planNameKey`, and the note that the swap will be part of the coming Business plan and every plan can use it until then — the owner's ask of 2026-09-07). The button opens a modal on `useModalDialog` (the desktop's one focus trap): the Business-plan callout in `FreeTierUpgradeCard`'s banner dress with an Upgrade link to `PORTAL_DASHBOARD_URL`, what the swap does (the harness installed from the internet, minutes; chat and assistant unavailable meanwhile; the ClawBox AI sign-in and the Telegram bot carried over; Telegram approvals and other providers per harness; the desktop reloads at the end; the same button swaps back — and the persona line said PER DIRECTION: "carries over" only towards Hermes, whose `hermes_edition` step seeds the shared identity from the OpenClaw workspace, while the way back runs no identity sync at all, so that bullet says OpenClaw keeps its own workspace's persona and memory and what was learned on Hermes stays there), Cancel and **Continue on the <plan>** — or, when `allowed` is false, the Upgrade link alone. Continue POSTs `{ harness }` and the SAME panel becomes the progress view: the six phases (request → install → lock → provision → carry → done) with the current one pulsing from the stream's `{phase}` lines, the last six `{status}` lines in a monospace pane (the step's own `[harness-swap] phase=…` markers are dropped from it), a "Running for m:ss" clock beside the no-cancel line (the Voice tab's counter, for its reason: the follow forwards one journal line per poll and a pip install is minutes of silence), no Cancel and Escape inert (a root step cannot be called back), the heading — the dialog's accessible name — said per stage ("Switching to…", "Switched to…", or the plain title over a failure, never "Switching…" over a red sentence), and focus moved onto the stage's one control, or onto the `tabIndex={-1}` panel while there is none, the moment the Continue button unmounts under it. The closing line: with NO `notes`, "Done — reloading…" held `SUCCESS_RELOAD_HOLD_MS` (1.5 s) before `window.location.reload()` (the client caches the edition for the page's life, so nothing short of a reload lands the desktop on the other harness); WITH notes — the facts the owner must act on, which nothing on the box shows again after the reload — no timer at all: they stay up behind a **Reload now** button, because an owner who walked away during a minutes-long install would otherwise have come back to a desktop that had already thrown them away. An `{error}` is the sentence with Close (focused), after which the card re-reads the box — a `code` the catalogue knows (`busy`, `coding_run_live`, `offline`, `disk`, `memory`, `plan_required`, `lock_unchanged`) is said in the owner's language, any other in the route's own words (`swap_failed` carries the step's sentence naming the phase and what the box now is, which no catalogue line could) — and a stream that ends with no verdict is the component's own `stream_lost`, "lost the connection… it may still be going", never "the swap failed", because the root unit runs on without the web server. `inProgress` from the GET draws an amber "A swap to <name> is in progress" row in place of the button and polls the GET every `IN_PROGRESS_POLL_MS` (5 s) until it flips, then reloads — the row is what a second tab, or a tab whose stream was lost, lands on, and read once it outlived the swap on a page whose cached edition was wrong; `swappable: false` (dual, an unknown edition) or a server without the route keeps the read-only badge; the unlocked-dual switcher is untouched. The status route and the swap GET are read together and set together, so the page never flashes the badge before the tiles. Its strings are `settings.harnessTagline*` / `settings.harnessThisBox` / `settings.harnessSwap*` — 46 keys, in all ten locales, pinned by `harness-picker.test.tsx`; the closing `notes` themselves are the route's English sentences, the one text on the panel the catalogue cannot word until a `code` travels beside each. On System, the Agent databases card (`DbMaintenancePanel.tsx`, not on the Hermes edition) sits between the device figures and the password card, outside the stats ternary: OpenClaw's SQLite databases with their sizes and unused share, which ones the next maintenance run would compact, the last run (before → after), the nightly switch and time, and **Run now** — `GET|POST /setup-api/gateway/db-maintenance`, owner only; the run itself is `scripts/openclaw-db-maintenance.py maintain` (harness-openclaw.md, the agent databases); strings `dbMaintenance.*` in all ten locales. On System, the Desktop & Power card (`SystemProfilePanel`) sits UNDER the password card, at the owner's request (2026-09-07) — the figures are what the page is opened for. System Update is the `update` section: it embeds `SystemUpdateApp` (`embedded` prop — the same component the `system_update` desktop window and `/app/system_update` render on their own), and About's update tile switches to it; the beta toggle, the branch pin and the update overlay that Settings used to carry itself live only there now. Appearance no longer has a "your name" field: the assistant asks for the name in its first conversation and writes `ui_user_name` through `preferences_set`, so it is the only writer and a text input that fought a 5-second poll for the same value earned its removal; the Language selector moved into that card's place. Local AI is the inventory alone (`LocalAiPanel.tsx`, the owner's ruling of 2026-09-15): one row per engine — Gemma on llama.cpp, Kokoro, Whisper, the memory embedder — each carrying the SAME four verbs as its own buttons, Install when it is absent (`llamacpp/install`, `tts/install`, `whisper {action:"install-engine"}`, `embed/install`, every one streamed), otherwise Enable/Disable (the unit switch on `local-models`; for Gemma, wiring it as the fallback and "turn off Local AI") and Uninstall behind a `ConfirmDialog` (`DELETE llamacpp/models?engine=1`, `DELETE tts/install`, `DELETE whisper?scope=engine`, `DELETE embed/install` — Kokoro and Whisper take the weights AND the engine's stamp and stop, disable and delete their user unit (`uninstallKokoro`, `uninstallWhisperEngine` through `removeUserUnit`), and the Whisper removal also takes its row out of the channel audio list and settles `stt_primary` on the cloud; the embedder DELETE stops `clawbox-embed.service` (stop, never disable — the unit, the llama.cpp binary and the index are install.sh's) and unlinks the GGUF only; the Gemma DELETE is refused 409 `in_use` while Gemma is the PRIMARY model or local-only mode is on, otherwise turns Local AI off through the panel's own Disable when the model is wired (else just stops the runtime) and unlinks the blessed GGUF — there is no Gemma stamp and no unit to remove; the Gemma dialog says the next update re-caches it, and a Kokoro removal settles a standing "this box" voice pick on Auto and answers the tts route's `fallback` shape), with the role actions (make primary, use as fallback, wake now) behind the "more" menu; the Whisper size picker and the embedder's "download again" are drawn under a row only while that engine is installed AND enabled. The ClawBox AI cloud card, the Ollama card, the GGUF library and the "Anything you disable…" footer are gone from it, so the only radiogroup that page ever had is gone with it and adding an Ollama model is a Terminal job or a setup-wizard one again. The ClawBox AI half of what that cloud card said is on PROVIDERS now, as `ClawboxAiPitchCard.tsx` (`clawai-pitch-card`, the owner's decision of 2026-09-15): a box whose `clawaiConfigured` is an EXPLICIT `false` — never a falsy test, or a subscriber sees a subscribe pitch on every open while the first read is in flight, and a REFUSED credential is still a configured one the connect panel below is what fixes — is led with the offer (what a subscription runs, **Connect ClawBox AI** through the offer counter Settings already hands `AIModelsStep`, and the portal's plans in a tab of its own) and, stated plainly under it rather than hidden behind it, the other route: the box runs its own smaller, slower models with nothing leaving it, and **Set up Local AI** opens that tab. Not amber and no warning glyph on it — a box with no subscription is not broken. Its strings are `settings.clawaiPitch.*` in a surface module of their own (`en-clawai-pitch.ts`) and in all ten locales, the namespace pinned by `edition-translations.test.ts`. Voice (`VoiceOutputPanel.tsx`) draws an amber block when the box HAS channels and cannot speak on them in its own voice (`channels.voiceNoteReady === false` asked explicitly, never a falsy test, so a status from a server that predates the field invents no warning), under the spoken-replies switch and only while it is on, with a button that POSTs `tts/install` and keeps only the NDJSON stream's closing verdict — a repair is one fact, not a build log — before re-reading the box, and a seconds counter because an apt transaction on an Orin is minutes. The repair lives here because a shipped box has no other route to it: Local AI's Install only appears on a Kokoro that is MISSING, and this box's is not. Its strings are `settings.voice.channelVoiceNotes` / `…Fix` / `…Installing` / `…Failed`, in all ten locales
