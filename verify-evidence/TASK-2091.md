## D: what broke the restart reattach

Commit `06148f4ad` (2026-10-27) changed the runs-file flush to every 5 s while
a run streams (`STREAM_FLUSH_MS`). `spawnRun` records the scope unit and the
process group in memory. It never wrote them out itself and waited for the
next stream flush. If `clawbox-setup` restarted within 5 s of a spawn, the
record on disk said `running` with `unit: null`. `reconcileAfterRestart` then
treated the run as lost and merged and removed its worktree while the scope
was still running. s8-restart-mid-run restarts 5 s into a run.
