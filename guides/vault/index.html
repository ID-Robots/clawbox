Everyday Use

ClawBox Vault

ClawBox Vault keeps your website logins on the box itself, encrypted. It fills them into login pages in the box’s own browser, and the assistant can use them to sign in to a site for you. The assistant never sees a password: the vault types it into the page, and the assistant only hears whether it worked. It is for the owner of the box, and it works the same on OpenClaw and on Hermes.
ClawBox Vault is the owner’s. Other users of the box do not see the app and cannot open it.

Before you start

  • Sign in to the box as its owner.
  • Nothing else. The vault is already on every box, on OpenClaw and on Hermes. There is nothing to install and no plan to buy.

Set a vault PIN

The vault does nothing until you choose a PIN. Until then it saves no login, fills none, and the assistant cannot use it.
1

Open ClawBox Vault

On the desktop, click ClawBox Vault (the green key). The window asks you to choose a vault PIN.
2

Type a PIN twice

Type a PIN of at least 6 characters in Vault PIN, and the same PIN again in Repeat the PIN. Choose one you will remember: you need it each time you open the vault.
3

Click Set PIN and open

The vault opens on Entries. It is empty for now: No entries yet. Add one, or save a login from the browser.
4

Check that the browser is connected

Click Browser on the left. At first it says Waiting for this box’s browser. The page does not update by itself: wait a minute, then click Browser again. It then says Extension connected (the assistant can fill logins). That is all: you do not install anything in the browser.

Save a login while you sign in

The box has its own browser, the one the assistant uses. You see it in the Agent Browser app. When you sign in to a site there, the vault offers to save the login.
1

Open Agent Browser

On the desktop, click Agent Browser. The window shows the box’s own browser. If no browser window is there, click Open Browser at the top.
2

Sign in to a site

Go to the site’s login page and sign in as you always do, with your username and password.
3

Click Save on the vault's bar

After you sign in, a dark bar appears at the top right of the page: Save to ClawBox Vault? It shows your username, the site, and an Entry name you can change. Click Save. The button says Saved and the bar closes.
4

Find it in the vault

Open ClawBox Vault. The login is now listed under Entries.
The browser may also ask Save password? in its own white panel under the address bar. That keeps the password in the browser, not in the vault. Click Not now there, and use the vault’s bar instead.

Add a login by hand

1

Click New entry

In ClawBox Vault, on the Entries page, click New entry.
2

Fill in the login

Type a Name (for example shop.example.com/ivan), your Login (username or email) and the URL of the site’s login page. Type the Password, or click Generate to have the vault make a strong one.
3

Click Save

A message says Saved shop.example.com/ivan. and the login is in the list.
To read a saved password, click the login in the list, then Reveal or Copy. The Generator page on the left also makes passwords: Use in new entry starts a new login with the one it made.

Fill a login yourself

1

Open the login page

In Agent Browser, go to the site’s login page.
2

Open the vault in the browser

Click the puzzle piece (Extensions) at the top right of the browser, then click ClawBox Vault. A small vault window opens. Under This site it lists the logins you saved for this site.
3

Click Fill

Click Fill next to your login. The vault types your username and password into the page, and its window closes.
4

Sign in

Click the site’s own sign in button.

Let the assistant sign in for you

This works on both editions, OpenClaw and Hermes.
1

Save the login first

The vault must already hold your login for that site. Save it while you sign in, or add it by hand (see above).
2

Ask in the chat

Open Chat and ask, for example: Log in to billing.example.com with my saved login from ClawBox Vault. Naming the vault helps the assistant pick the right tool.
3

Read the answer

The assistant opens the site in the box’s browser and asks the vault to fill your login. The vault types the password into the page itself. The assistant then tells you that you are signed in, or why it could not sign in. Open Agent Browser if you want to see the page.
The assistant can also make a login for a new account. It asks the vault for a strong password, the vault stores it, and the vault types it into the sign up form. You can read that password in the app whenever you need it. The assistant never sees it.

What the assistant can and cannot do

It canIt cannot
See which sites and usernames you savedSee, read or copy any password
Ask the vault to fill a login into that site’s own pageFill a login into a different site’s page
Save a new login, or delete one after asking youOpen the vault app or learn your PIN

See what happened

Every fill, save and delete is written down in the vault, with the login and the site. Passwords are never written there.
1

Open Activity

In ClawBox Vault, click Activity on the left. The newest lines are at the top.
2

Read the lines

Who says who did it: ui is you in the vault app, agent is the assistant, extension is the box’s browser, and system is the vault itself. Action, Entry and Site say what happened, to which login, on which site. For a fill by the assistant, Result says filled when it worked, or why it did not, for example locked.
3

Click Refresh

Refresh at the top right shows lines added since you opened the page.

Lock the vault, the browser, or change the PIN

Lock the vault app
  1. Click Lock vault at the bottom left. The window shows Locked. and asks for your PIN.
  2. Type your PIN in Vault PIN and click Unlock to open it again.
The vault app also locks itself after 15 minutes without use. Stop all fills in the browser for a while
  1. In ClawBox Vault, click Browser.
  2. Click Lock extension. A message says Extension locked: no fills until it is unlocked. The assistant cannot sign in anywhere now either, and the vault does not offer to save logins.
  3. To undo it, click Unlock extension on the same page.
Disconnect the browser from the vault
  1. In ClawBox Vault, click Browser, then Unpair.
  2. Confirm with OK. The page says Unpaired. The browser stays out until you pair it again. It stays that way, also after a restart.
  3. To connect it again, click Pair this box’s browser. It connects within a minute.
Change the PIN
  1. Click Change PIN at the bottom left.
  2. Type your Current PIN, then the new PIN twice.
  3. Click Change PIN. A message says PIN changed.

Keep a copy of your passwords

ClawKeep does not back up the vault. Your passwords stay on this box and nowhere else. If you want a copy, you have to make it yourself: see Make your own copy below.

If it does not work

No Save to ClawBox Vault? bar after I sign in

  • No PIN yet. The vault offers to save nothing until it has a PIN. Open ClawBox Vault and choose one.
  • You signed in somewhere else. The vault lives only in the box’s own browser, the one in Agent Browser. It is not in the browser on your computer or phone.
  • The browser is not connected or is locked. Open ClawBox Vault, click Browser, and check that it says Extension connected and Extension is unlocked.
  • The page does not start with https://. The vault does not save or fill on plain http:// pages, except pages on the box itself.
  • The vault already has that login. It only asks again when the password changed, and then the bar says Update password?.

The Browser page does not say Extension connected

  • Waiting for this box’s browser or Extension not connected right now (is the browser open?): open Agent Browser, click Open Browser if no browser window is there, wait a minute, then click Browser again to refresh the page.
  • Unpaired. The browser stays out until you pair it again.: click Pair this box’s browser and wait a minute.
  • Extension is locked: click Unlock extension.

The assistant says the vault is locked, not set up, or has no login for the site

  • Locked: open ClawBox Vault, click Browser, click Unlock extension, then ask again.
  • Not set up: the vault has no PIN yet. Open ClawBox Vault and choose one, then save the login.
  • No login saved for that site: save the login first, then ask again. Check that the site you named is the one the login belongs to.
  • The browser is not running: open Agent Browser, then ask again.

I forgot my vault PIN

Your saved logins are not lost. Only the PIN is. You can remove the PIN and choose a new one: this takes a few commands in Terminal, see Reset a forgotten PIN below. If you would rather not, contact ClawBox support (see Need a hand? on the home page).A wrong PIN shows wrong PIN. After five wrong tries the vault asks you to wait: Too many attempts. Try again in 30 s. Each further wrong try makes the wait longer.

Details

What the assistant gets back

The vault never gives the assistant a password. When it asks the vault to fill a login, it gets one status back, and it tells you what that means:
StatusMeaning
filledThe login was typed into the page.
no_formThe open page has no login form.
no_tabThe page is not open in the box’s browser.
origin_mismatchThe form belongs to another site, sits in another site’s frame, or the page is plain http://.
no_entryNo saved login matches the site.
lockedThe extension is locked (Browser → Lock extension, or Lock in the vault’s window in the browser).
extension_offlineThe box’s browser is closed, or it is unpaired.
timeoutThe browser did not answer within 20 seconds.
errorThe browser could not finish the fill.
Before the vault has a PIN, every request gets “not set up” instead, and the assistant tells you to choose a PIN.

Which pages the vault fills

  • Only pages served over https://. Plain http:// is allowed only for pages on the box itself (localhost).
  • Only the site a login belongs to. A login for example.com also fits login.example.com; a login saved for the exact address is offered first.
  • Only the page itself, or a frame inside it with exactly the same address as the page. A login form inside a frame from any other address is refused.

Passwords on screen and on the clipboard

  • In the app, a password shows only after Reveal and hides again after 30 seconds.
  • Copy puts it on the clipboard, which is cleared after 30 seconds while the vault’s page stays open.
  • Payment cards are not part of the vault. It keeps website logins only.

Where the vault keeps your logins

Everything is in the folder ~/.clawbox-vault on the box:
  • store: your logins, each one encrypted.
  • gnupg: the vault’s own key, which decrypts them.
  • state.json: the PIN (stored as a hash, never in plain text) and the browser’s pairing.
The Files app, the assistant’s file and command tools and the Coding Agent are not allowed into this folder. A factory reset does not remove it.

Make your own copy

Copy the folders ~/.clawbox-vault/store and ~/.clawbox-vault/gnupg to a safe place yourself, for example over SSH. You need both: the first holds your passwords, and the second is the key that opens them. Together they are your passwords, so keep that copy as safe as the passwords themselves.

Reset a forgotten PIN

This removes the PIN and keeps every saved login. You need the box password (the one you sign in to the box with).
  1. Open Terminal on the desktop, or connect to the box over SSH.
  2. Stop the vault. Type the box password when you are asked: 
    sudo systemctl stop clawbox-vault
  3. Remove the PIN: 
    python3 -c "import json,os; p=os.path.expanduser('~/.clawbox-vault/state.json'); d=json.load(open(p)); d.pop('pin',None); json.dump(d,open(p,'w'),indent=2)"
  4. Start the vault again: 
    sudo systemctl start clawbox-vault
  5. Open ClawBox Vault. It asks you to choose a PIN, as on the first day. Your logins are still under Entries, and the browser stays connected.
