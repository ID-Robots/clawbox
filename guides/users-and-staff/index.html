Everyday Use

Users & Staff

Give other people their own sign in on your ClawBox. Each person gets their own password and their own chat. Turn on Organisation mode when the box serves a company’s staff: each member of staff then gets only a chat, their own files and their own settings, and you keep everything else. This page is for the owner of the box.

Before you start

  • Sign in as the owner, the account you set the box up with. Only the owner sees the Users tab.
  • Everything on this page works on both editions, OpenClaw and Hermes, unless a line names one of them. None of it needs a ClawBox AI plan.
  • Add only people you trust with the box.

Add a person

1

Open Settings

Open Settings from the desktop or the shelf.
2

Open the Users tab

Choose System in the list on the left, then the Users tab at the top. You see your own account, marked Owner and You. Before you add anyone, the list says No other users yet.
3

Type a username

Under Add a user, type a name in Username, for example ivan. Use lower case letters, digits, - and _, up to 32 characters, and start with a letter. A few names are kept for the system, such as admin; the form says so under the field.
4

Type a password

Type a password of at least 8 characters in Choose a password, and the same again in Repeat the password.
5

Create the account

Press Create user. You see ivan can now sign in. and the person appears in the list, with Reset password and Remove beside their name.
6

Tell the person

Give them the username and the password. They open the address of your ClawBox in their browser and sign in.

Turn on organisation mode

Leave organisation mode off for family, or for a small team you trust with the whole box. Then everyone you add gets the same apps as you, apart from updating the box and the assistant’s own consoles. On OpenClaw they also get its console while you share OpenClaw with all users. Turn it on for a company’s staff.
1

Find the switch

In Settings → System → Users, scroll to the Organisation mode card.
2

Turn it on

Turn the switch on. The line under the text now starts with On and says that users see Chat, Files and their own settings.
3

Let your staff reload

The change applies at once. Each person’s desktop changes the next time it loads. A Terminal or remote desktop they still had open closes.
Every user other than you is now a member of staff. You keep everything else, including the AI providers and the system settings.

What a member of staff sees

This is the same on OpenClaw and on Hermes.
1

Open the box

The person opens the address of your ClawBox in their browser.
2

Sign in

They type their name in Username and their password in Password, then press Log In. On an organisation box the login page asks for a username instead of listing everyone. You leave Username empty to sign in as the owner.
3

Their desktop

They see three apps: Chat, Files and Settings. Nothing else.
  • Chat runs on an assistant of their own, with its own memory that nobody else reads. It answers questions, reads the files they attach and writes files into their own folder. It cannot browse the web, send messages, run commands or change the box. If they ask it to search the web, it tells them it cannot.
  • Files shows two folders: Home, their own home folder, and Shared, one folder that every member of staff and you can use. You see it as a folder named Shared in your own files.
  • Settings has only Appearance (language, wallpaper and look) and Account, where they change their own password.
  • Their chats use the AI provider you set up for the box.

Set limits

Limits for other users sets what everyone but you may use. You are never limited, and a ClawBox with only you on it has nobody they apply to. The limits apply to the people you added, also while organisation mode is off.
1

Find the limits

In Settings → System → Users, scroll to Limits for other users.
2

Change a number

Type a new number in a box. Type 0 to turn that limit off.
3

Save

Press Save limits. You see Saved. The limits apply at once.
4

Check the space used

Under Home folder usage you see how much each person’s home folder holds, for example 17.8 KB of 10 GB. A person who has not opened Files yet shows Not measured until they do.
LimitDefaultWhat happens past it
Largest file per upload or chat attachment100 MBThe upload is refused and the person is told the limit. A chat attachment stays within the chat’s own 25 MB limit as well.
Home folder quota per user10 GBAn upload, or a move from Shared into Home, that would go past it is refused. Deleting files always works.
Chat messages per user, per minute20The message is not sent.
Chat messages per user, per hour300The same, counted over the last hour.

Give your staff tools

Your assistant can use MCP servers: services that give it extra tools, such as a document search. You choose which of them your staff may use. This works on OpenClaw and on Hermes. Before you start, the server must already be added to your assistant. If the card says The assistant has no MCP server yet, add one first.
1

Find the card

In Settings → System → Users, scroll to Tools for members of staff. It lists every MCP server your assistant has.
2

Tick a server

Tick Members of staff may use followed by the server’s name. More choices open below it.
3

Choose who

Choose Every member of staff, or Only these members of staff: and tick the people who should have it.
4

Decide about credentials

Leave Each member uses their own credential ticked for a service where every person signs in as themselves, such as a document search or a Microsoft 365 gateway. Untick it for a tool on your ClawBox that needs no sign in, such as a local “create a Word document” tool.
5

Allow writing, if you want to

Tick Also allow its tools that create or edit if your staff should be able to write with it, for example a document. Without the tick, its tools that create or edit stay blocked.
6

Save

Press Save. You see Saved. The change applies to each member’s assistant straight away.
7

Set each person's credential

If you left Each member uses their own credential ticked, a Credentials list appears with one row per person, for example ivan on documents. Type their token or key in Token or key and press Set. The row then shows the credential hidden behind dots, with the last four characters of a long one. A person whose row says Not set gets no tools from that server.
When a member’s assistant uses one of these tools to write a file, the reply shows it as a download card. Only that member can open it.

Change a password or remove a person

Reset a password

1

Open the form

In Settings → System → Users, press Reset password beside the person. A form opens with the title New password for maria.
2

Type the new password

Type it in Choose a password and again in Repeat the password.
3

Save it

Press Set password. You see maria’s password was reset. They are signed out everywhere and sign in again with the new password.

Remove a person

1

Press Remove

Press Remove beside the person. The box asks Remove maria? and says that their account, home folder and files are deleted.
2

Confirm

Press Remove user. You see maria was removed. They are signed out, and their account, their home folder and their files are gone from the box.

Read the audit log

1

Open the log

In Settings → System → Users, scroll to Audit log. The newest entries are at the top.
2

Load more

Press Refresh to see what happened since you opened the page. Press Show older entries to go further back.
The log lists who signed in, failed to sign in or signed out, and from which address. It also lists what was done on the box: users added and removed, passwords reset or changed, organisation mode and OpenClaw sharing switched on or off, the limits changed, the owner’s password changed, updates started, the assistant’s runtime changed, and a factory reset. Only you can read it.

If it does not work

A member of staff gets no tool from a server. Open Tools for members of staff and check three things. The server is ticked and the person is included. If the server uses a credential, the person’s row does not say Not set. The server does not say Cannot be offered to staff. If it does, the reason follows: most often the server is not a Streamable HTTP server (see Details below). Tools also work only while organisation mode is on. While it is off, the card says Takes effect while organisation mode is on. An upload is refused. Files names the file and says it is larger than the upload limit for their account, or says Your home folder is full. Raise Largest file per upload or chat attachment or Home folder quota per user in Limits for other users, or ask the person to delete files they no longer need. A message gets no answer, or is refused. The person has sent more messages than Limits for other users allows. On OpenClaw the chat says You have reached your chat limit on this ClawBox, with how many messages it allows and how long to wait. On Hermes the message can stay without an answer and no time is shown: wait a minute, or up to an hour if the hourly limit was reached. Then send it again. To allow more, raise Chat messages per user, per minute or Chat messages per user, per hour.

Details

Only MCP servers that use the Streamable HTTP transport can be offered to staff. On OpenClaw, an entry that has a url but no transport counts as SSE: add "transport": "streamable-http" to it in openclaw.json. On Hermes, a server that runs a local command, or uses SSE, cannot be offered: give it the url of a Streamable HTTP endpoint, without a command, in config.yaml, and remove transport: sse and any cwd (working folder) the entry has. Changing the entry does not change the server: a server that only speaks SSE still cannot be offered. A plain-HTTP address works only for a server on the ClawBox itself (127.0.0.1, not localhost). Anything else needs HTTPS. A server whose address carries a credential cannot be offered either.
Which tools staff get. A server you do not tick is never offered to anyone on your staff. The list shows every MCP server your assistant has, on OpenClaw and on Hermes alike. A server’s tools that create or edit stay blocked for staff unless you tick Also allow its tools that create or edit. Tools that send, publish, pay, delete or run something stay blocked either way. Your own credentials for the server are never sent on a member’s behalf. Credential header names the header that carries a member’s credential; it is Authorization unless you change it. Files the assistant makes for a member of staff. When a member’s assistant uses a granted tool to write a file, the reply shows it as a download card. Only that member can open it. Another member who asks for it by its exact path gets “not found”. As the owner, you can still open every file in the chat media folder, as before. A file counts as the member’s when:
  • a tool their own assistant called created it in the assistant’s chat media folder during that call, and the tool’s answer named it (a file that already existed before the call never counts); or
  • their assistant wrote it into their own assistant folder during their conversation turn.
A file in the shared chat media folder never becomes a member’s just because their assistant’s reply mentions it. A path anywhere else on the box, a link that points elsewhere, or a file that already belongs to another member is never handed out this way. Grants are kept per assistant. If your ClawBox can switch between OpenClaw and Hermes, a grant you made on one does not carry over to a server with the same name on the other. Granting that name on the other one moves the grant there, and the credentials set for it are cleared. The audit log. A failed sign in names the account only when it exists on this ClawBox. Nothing on the box edits an entry. A factory reset empties the log and then records the reset. The log is kept in the ClawBox data folder, in its current file and one older one, about 10 MB together. Beyond that, the oldest entries are dropped. The quota. The quota counts each person’s own home folder. While organisation mode is on, Files shows it to them as Home. Writes into Shared do not count against anyone’s quota. A member’s memory. On OpenClaw, a member’s assistant can also search the folders you indexed for Memory Shard. Check those folders before you turn organisation mode on. What else changes. With organisation mode on, ClawBox AI offers are hidden for everyone, you included. Share OpenClaw with all users, a switch on the Users tab of an OpenClaw ClawBox, does not reach members of staff. Spoken replies follow your own Voice setting.
