Everyday Use

Chat

Your ClawBox has two chat surfaces on the desktop, and they talk to the same assistant:
  • the chat panel, which opens when you click the mascot and docks beside your windows;
  • the Chat app, a full window from the desktop icon.
Everything on this page is on both editions unless it says otherwise.
On the Hermes edition the chat shows the Hermes icon where these pictures show the crab.

The pills: provider, model and reasoning

The chat panel carries up to three pills in the row beneath the message box.
PillWhat it does
ProviderWhich service answers. Picking a different one switches the conversation to it.
ModelWhich of that provider’s models. The pill drops the vendor’s name because the pill to its left already says it — the dropdown shows the full model name.
Reasoning effortHow hard the model thinks before answering. The levels on offer are whatever the provider accepts, so this control changes shape: a provider that takes a scale shows it, one that only has thinking on or off shows two states, and one that takes no reasoning level at all does not show the control.
A change applies to the running agent. There is no restart between you and the next message, and open conversations are not dropped. On OpenClaw, changing the default model in Settings → Providers, installing or switching Local AI, and the local-only switch are applied live too wherever they can be, so the chat carries on.
On ClawBox AI there is no model pill — the chat names the provider and there is nothing to choose. The reasoning control is there and starts at Medium.
A reasoning change is a per-conversation override. It does not rewrite the box’s default, which lives in Settings → Providers.

Slash commands

Type / in the composer and a menu appears with the commands your assistant actually publishes — the built-in ones plus anything your skills and plugins added. Each row shows the command, its arguments if it takes any, and the agent’s own one-line description. Keep typing to filter; pick a row to insert it. The list is read from the agent itself, so it is never out of date with what your box can actually run, and it differs between editions and between boxes with different skills installed.

When the assistant needs you

Three kinds of card can appear in the transcript. All three mean the assistant has stopped and is waiting for you.

A question

The assistant asks you to choose. Each option carries its own short description — usually the consequence of picking it — and there is a field for an answer of your own. One Send answer submits the whole card. There is no skip: to decline, leave it, and the assistant carries on when its own timer runs out.

An approval

The assistant is asking to do something that needs your permission. Allow once, Always allow or Deny. Nothing happens until you answer, and if nobody answers in time the window closes and it does not go ahead.

Email waiting to be sent

The assistant wrote a message and wants to send it from your account. You read it first and nothing goes out until you approve — this card is what Ask me before sending in Settings → Channels → Email produces, and it is on by default. You can send them all, send one, or delete them without sending. See Messaging channels.

When a message fails

A failed turn says what actually went wrong: the provider’s own reason, not a generic “the run failed”. Two common cases have their own wording:
  • A fallback model answered. Your first-choice model refused or errored and the next model in the chain replied instead. The reply says which model wrote it.
  • An allowance ran out. On ClawBox AI, the chat says whether it was the weekly chat allowance, the short-term limit (5 hours) or the memory-indexing allowance, and when it frees up — for the weekly allowance also how to add an extra-usage pack. Your usage is in Settings → Providers.
  • The sign-in is missing or revoked. When the box has no working sign-in for the provider the chat is set to, the chat says so and names the two fixes: connect that provider again in Settings → Providers, or pick another model in the header. A Claude sign-in that needs renewing is marked on the Providers strip too. A ClawBox AI turn that failed on its sign-in offers a way to sign in again. See Troubleshooting.
  • An Anthropic account hit its limit. With several Anthropic accounts on OpenClaw, the next message moves to another account instead of answering with the limit message.

Stopping a reply

Stop ends the reply that is being written. Whatever part of the answer had arrived stays, with a grey Stopped. line under it, so a stopped reply is not mistaken for a lost one. On Hermes the stopped reply is kept after a reload too.

Long conversations

A conversation keeps growing until it no longer fits what the model can read at once:
  • On OpenClaw with ClawBox AI, a long chat is shortened (compacted) automatically, so each message does not send more and more of the conversation.
  • On the on-device model, a conversation that grows too long offers a new chat instead of a minutes-long shortening on the box. The card says how much room the model has.

Voice

Voice in — in the chat panel, hold the microphone button while you speak and let go to send: the recording is turned into text and sent as a message. A quick click still starts and stops a recording instead. Holding F8 while the chat is open does the same, and Esc cancels. The Chat app has no microphone. Recording needs a secure connection, so on a plain http:// address the chat points you at this box’s Remote Access tunnel instead of failing silently. Voice out — Settings → Voice → Spoken replies, off by default. With it on, a reply gets a player on its bubble: press play, scrub through it, or download the audio. A question you asked out loud is answered out loud. Where your box answers messaging channels, a voice note there gets a spoken reply too. Two more switches in Settings → Voice, both off by default:
  • Auto-play every spoken reply plays the reply to a question you typed aloud as well, while you are at the chat.
  • Short spoken answers has a spoken reply say only the main answer, in at most three sentences, so it starts sooner. The full reply still appears in the chat.
Spoken replies download compressed — a 15-second reply is about 90 KB. When the cloud voice does not answer, a reply gives up after about 20 seconds, and a short line under it says the voice is not answering or could not speak that reply.
Speaking on Telegram, WhatsApp and Discord is an OpenClaw-edition feature. On the Hermes edition the box still answers out loud in the chat; replies on those channels stay in text.

Other things in the composer

  • Attach a picture or take a photo, and ask about it, or attach a file. On Hermes, documents reach the assistant even when its model cannot look at pictures.
  • Generate a picture from a description.
  • Several chats at once, in the chat panel — tabs across the top, each its own conversation. New session starts a fresh one in the same tab.
  • Files the assistant produced appear as cards you can download, with their real name and size.

Word, Excel and PowerPoint files

You can attach a Word, Excel or PowerPoint file in the chat and ask about it. The box takes the text out of the file and hands it to the assistant, so the answer comes from what the file says. You can also ask the assistant to make such a file for you. It is for anyone who uses the chat, on both editions, OpenClaw and Hermes.

Before you attach a file

  • The file is a .docx (Word), .xlsx (Excel), .pptx (PowerPoint), .odt (OpenDocument text) or .ods (OpenDocument spreadsheet). An older .doc, .xls or .ppt file is not read: open it on your computer and save it in the newer format first.
  • The file is smaller than 10 MB.
  • The file has no password.

Ask about a file

1

Click Attach file

In the chat, click Attach file (the paperclip in the row under the message box). On a phone, tap + first, then Attach file. Your device’s file picker opens.
2

Pick the file

Pick one file, or several. The name of each file shows above the message box. Click the × beside a name to take that file off again.
3

Ask your question and send it

Type what you want to know, for example What does this offer cost in all?, and send it. The assistant answers from the text of the file. A spreadsheet reaches it as one table per sheet, so you can ask about a sheet by its name.

Ask the assistant to make a file

1

Ask for the file in plain words

Say what should be in it and which kind of file you want, for example Make a short Word document with a thank-you note to Acme Ltd and send me the file.
2

Wait for the card

When the file is ready, the answer has a card with the name of the file and a download button. Making a file takes longer than a plain answer.
3

Download the file

Click the download button on the card. Your browser saves the file like any other download.
Word and Excel files need nothing extra. The first time you ask for a PowerPoint file, the assistant first installs a small tool for it, so that answer takes longer and the box has to be online.

If a file does not work

  • … could not be read, so it was not attached. The box could not get the text out of the file. The same line says why:
    • The file is password-protected, so its text cannot be read. Save a copy without a password and attach that.
    • The file is damaged or is not really a Word, Excel, PowerPoint or OpenDocument file. Open it on your computer, save it again as .docx, .xlsx or .pptx, and attach the new copy.
    • The file unpacks to more than the box reads from one document. Split it, or save the part you need on its own.
  • … could not be attached. Upload failed: Unexpected end of form: the file is bigger than about 10 MB. Make it smaller, for example by saving it without its pictures or by splitting it, and attach it again.
  • … is too large to attach. This file is larger than the … upload limit for your account on this ClawBox. You are not the owner, and the owner has set a lower limit for your account. Only the owner can change it: see Users & Staff.
  • The assistant says the document is empty. The file holds only pictures or empty pages. The box reads the text of a document, not the pictures in it. To ask about a picture, attach the picture itself.
  • The assistant says it cannot open the file, or answers without reading it. Ask again and tell it to read the attached file first. A small on-device model misses files more often than a cloud model: pick another one with the Model pill on a phone, or the provider pill on a computer.

The chat on a phone held upright

On a phone held upright, the chat fills the screen. Its controls fit into one slim bar at the top and one message box at the bottom, so the conversation gets the room. It is for anyone who chats with the box from a phone, on both editions, OpenClaw and Hermes. A phone held sideways, a tablet and a computer show the chat as before.

Before you start on a phone

  • The box runs ClawBox 4.4 or newer.
  • Open the box’s address in your phone’s browser and sign in. On a phone the box opens straight into the chat.

Find your way around

1

Hold the phone upright

At the top is one slim bar: Menu (three lines) on the left and, once the chat has messages, the chat’s name in the middle. At the bottom is one rounded message box. Under the text are +, the model pill (for example ClawBox Medium), the microphone and Send. While the assistant writes, Send turns into Stop.
2

Tap Menu

The menu slides in from the left. It has New chat, your Chats, Desktop and Text size. Tap a chat to open it. The chat you are in has a button on its right: × closes that chat, and on the main ClawBox chat a round arrow starts a new session instead. The × at the top closes the menu.
3

Change the text size, or go to the desktop

In the menu, A− and A+ make the text of the chat smaller or larger. Tap the percentage between them to go back to 100%. Desktop closes the chat and shows the box’s desktop. To come back, tap the chat button on the shelf at the bottom.
4

Tap + to attach a file

Chat options slides up from the bottom, with Attach file and Create app. See Word, Excel and PowerPoint files for what to do next.
5

Tap the model pill to change the model

The Model sheet slides up from the bottom. It has the same choices as the pills on a computer: the Chat provider, its models, and the Reasoning effort (called Thinking for a Hermes model that can only turn thinking on or off). Picking a provider or a model closes the sheet; picking a reasoning level leaves it open.
6

Hold the microphone to talk

Hold it while you speak and let go to send, as described under Voice. This needs a secure (https) address: see below.

If the phone looks different

  • The chat has a header with its name and model at the top, and a settings button beside the message box, but no Menu. The phone is held sideways, or the screen is as wide as a tablet’s. Turn the phone upright, and turn off the browser’s desktop-site setting if it is on (that setting makes the page wider than 768 pixels). The phone layout needs a page narrower than 768 pixels: if the page is still that wide, ClawBox 4.4 shows the computer layout on purpose. Only if the page is narrower and it still looks the same does the box run a version older than 4.4: see Updating.
  • The microphone does not record. On a plain http:// address the chat shows Voice input works over a secure (HTTPS) address, and tapping the microphone opens Use the microphone over Remote Access. Turn on Remote Access in Settings → Remote Control (the Open Remote Control link takes you there), then open the box at its https address. You can also send voice messages from Telegram.

Coding runs in the chat

When the assistant hands work to the Coding Agent, the run shows as a card in the chat, and the chat that started it hears when it ends. A run started somewhere else — another browser or device — shows its card in the open chat within about half a minute, without a reload. A card you hide with × stays hidden, in every window and after a reload; Show finished coding runs again brings back only the cards that left on their own.

Where the conversation runs

The pills are per-conversation; the box’s default provider and model are in Settings → Providers, and that is what a new conversation starts on. When a sign-in has to restart the agent — connecting a subscription, for instance — the chats you already have open are re-pointed at the new default once the agent answers again, so a conversation cannot keep running on the old model while the pills name the new one. If that could not be done, the box tells you rather than leaving it to be discovered.

Details

Word, Excel and PowerPoint files in detail

  • What the box takes out. From a document: the paragraphs in order, and a table row by row with its cells separated by a bar (|). From a presentation: the text of each slide under its slide number. From a spreadsheet: each sheet as comma separated values under the sheet’s name, with the values the file stores, so a date is the spreadsheet’s date number and a formula is its last result.
  • When. The text is taken out once, when the file arrives, and saved on the box beside the file. A file whose text cannot be taken out is refused, and nothing of it stays on the box. A document with no text at all (only pictures or empty pages) is not refused: the assistant is told that it holds no text.
  • How it reaches the assistant. On Hermes the text goes into your message, up to 16 KB per file and 32 KB per message. Past that, the assistant is told where the rest is and opens it itself. On OpenClaw the message tells the assistant where the text is, and it reads it with its file tools.
  • How much is read. Up to 1,000,000 characters of text per document. A longer one is cut, with a note that the document goes on. A sheet is read up to 20,000 rows or 400,000 cells. A file that unpacks to more than 160 MB, or one part of it to more than 64 MB, is refused as too large.
  • File size. The chat’s own ceiling is 25 MB per file. On ClawBox 4.4 a file over about 10 MB stops on the way, with Upload failed: Unexpected end of form. Users other than the owner are also held to the owner’s Largest file per upload or chat attachment (100 MB unless the owner changes it) when it is the lower of the two.
  • Passwords. A Word, Excel or PowerPoint file with a password, or an OpenDocument file saved with encryption, is refused.
  • A dropped folder that already holds a file named like the saved text (the document’s name with .txt added) is refused, with a message to rename one of the two.
  • Making files. Word and Excel files are made with tools that come with the box. For a PowerPoint file the assistant installs a tool the first time it is asked. On both editions the finished file comes back as a download card in the chat.

The upright phone layout in detail

  • The layout is used when the browser window is narrower than 768 pixels and at least as tall as it is wide. A wider window, as on most tablets and on a large phone held sideways, gets the computer layout. A smaller phone held sideways keeps the earlier phone layout, with its own fullscreen choice, which the upright layout ignores.
  • The chats in the menu are the same list as the tabs on a computer, shared by every browser signed in to the box. A chat that is answering has an orange dot, and one with a reply you have not read has a green dot.
  • The menu and the two sheets each have an × to close them. The phone’s Back button closes an open menu or sheet before it closes the chat.
  • The Model sheet holds the same choices as the pills: the provider, that provider’s models, and the reasoning levels the provider takes.
  • On a Hermes box whose assistant cannot draw pictures itself, Chat options also has Generate a picture when the box is signed in to ClawBox AI. Members of staff on an organisation box do not get Create app.
