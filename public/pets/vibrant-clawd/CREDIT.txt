ClawBox crab (pack id `vibrant-clawd`)
======================================

ClawBox's own mascot, in Petdex pack format. This is FIRST-PARTY artwork —
drawn for this product by the ClawBox maintainer — which is why it may be
bundled here at all. Nothing in src/lib/pet-curated.ts changes: Petdex's
submitted art stays un-mirrored and is still downloaded on the owner's own
click, onto the owner's own device. The rule is "we do not redistribute other
people's sprites", not "no sprite ever ships"; the same distinction already
lets public/pet-egg-sheet.png ride along under its MIT notice.

Sheet geometry
--------------
1536x1872 px RGBA WebP: 8 columns x 9 rows of 192x208 cells — the nine-state
Petdex/Codex atlas src/lib/pet-state-map.ts already knows (CODEX_STATE_ROWS).

  row 0  idle           6 frames
  row 1  running-right  6 frames
  row 2  running-left   6 frames
  row 3  waving         4 frames
  row 4  jumping        5 frames
  row 5  failed         6 frames
  row 6  waiting        6 frames
  row 7  running        6 frames
  row 8  review         6 frames

Ragged rows are normal and are measured, not assumed: src/lib/pet-sheet-
metrics.ts scans the alpha channel once per sheet revision, so the tail cells
of `waving` and `jumping` are never stepped onto and the feet land on the bar.

Where it is used
----------------
  - the desktop mascot wherever the brand crab is worn (openclaw and dual
    editions with no pet picked, or the pet switched off) — hermes-pets.ts
    `brandPetSlug()`;
  - the pet gallery: the FIRST tile, named for the crab and selected by
    default, wherever the crab is the placeholder; an ordinary built-in,
    already-installed tile on a Hermes-only box;
  - public/clawbox-crab.png (and its inlined copy, and the docs-site copy) is
    a still of row 0 frame 0, cut by scripts/generate-crab-still.mjs.
