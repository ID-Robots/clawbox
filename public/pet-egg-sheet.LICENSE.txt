Original art credit
-------------------
Upstream's own source comment (apps/desktop/src/components/pet/
pixel-egg-sprite.tsx) credits the artwork as the iamcrog "bouncing hatching
egg" 12-frame sheet.

Sheet geometry
--------------
32x384 px, RGBA. 12 stacked cells of 32x32.
Frames 0-8 are the intact egg (two alternating squash/stretch poses).
Frames 9-11 are the crack/hatch burst.
ClawBox uses frames 0-5 only, so the placeholder egg never shows a crack.
