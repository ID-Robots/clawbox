What it’s good for

  • A 24/7 personal assistant you message from Telegram or the web, with additional OpenClaw channels available for advanced setups when supported.
  • Browser automation and everyday agent tasks.
  • Running the preinstalled on-device model offline, or cloud AI providers (ClawBox AI, Claude, GPT, Gemini) when you want heavier reasoning.

​
The local model it ships with

ClawBox arrives with a local model already installed and running, so it works offline out of the box with nothing to download. It is Google Gemma 4 E2B (the QAT Q4_0 build) served by llama.cpp, and it appears in the provider and chat pickers as Gemma 4 (on-device). No API key, no account, no internet.
Gemma 4 E2B (shipped default)
Generation~29 tok/s
Download / resident~3.1 GB / ~3.3 GB
Agentic tool use92.3%
Strict JSON100%
Measured on a Jetson Orin Nano 8GB against llama-server, the runtime the device actually ships. Why this build, and not a faster one. Four candidates from the same model family were benchmarked on the device. Three were faster — one reached 34.7 tok/s — and all four scored 100% on strict JSON. This one ships because it is the only one that reliably completes a two-step tool chain: look up the contact, then send the mail. The others stop halfway and ask you for the address they were sent to find. The larger E4B variant was rejected on the same grounds: 18.3 tok/s and 1.8 GB more memory for no gain in accuracy.
Installing Gemma 4 E2B from Ollama instead gives you a Q8_0 artifact of about 8.1 GB, which does not fit in 8 GB. The 3.1 GB above is the Q4_0 GGUF this device ships with.

Measured on a production ClawBox with Ollama (warm runs, num_predict=200, Ollama’s own eval_rate; power and thermals from tegrastats over 176 samples): ~11.4 W average, 19.2 W peak, 61.8 °C peak, no throttling. Most people read at 5–8 tok/s, so the 1–4B class generates faster than you can read. Full methodology: We Benchmarked a Production ClawBox. See full requirements and tiers in Hardware Requirements.

What to pair it with

ClawBox Connect shines as an always-on gateway. For running large local models (70B–200B parameters) on-device, see ClawBox Workstation.
