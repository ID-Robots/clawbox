ClawBox hardware is yours to own. A **ClawBox AI subscription** is optional and powers
the default managed AI experience plus higher support tiers.

## What a subscription adds

Managed **ClawBox AI** is available on every plan, including the free one — a
subscription buys **more of it**, a bigger backup allowance, and a higher support
tier.

<Note>
You can also run ClawBox with **your own** provider key or **local models**
instead of ClawBox AI — see [Choose Your AI Provider](/setup/choose-ai-provider).
The provider list depends on your edition: on [Hermes](/editions/hermes-providers)
it is OpenRouter, Anthropic, Google Gemini, z.ai/GLM and Kimi by key, plus
sign-in for OpenAI (Codex), GitHub Copilot and Nous Portal.
</Note>

## Plans

| Plan | Price | ClawBox AI | ClawKeep backups | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | €0, free forever | Managed ClawBox AI — standard daily usage, DeepSeek V4 Flash | 1 GB | Hardware, warranty, shipping, billing + **basic setup guidance** by email |
| **Pro** | €9 / month | 5× the Free usage, DeepSeek V4 Flash · Remote Desktop · priority processing | 5 GB | **Email support** for setup & configuration |
| **Max** | €49 / month (30-day trial) | Maximum usage, **DeepSeek V4 Pro (frontier)** · Remote Desktop · highest priority | 50 GB | **Full support — real humans via call/meeting** |

<Note>
Free and Pro both run DeepSeek V4 Flash; only **Max** configures the frontier
model. The plan you are on is shown on the ClawBox AI card in **Settings → AI
Provider** and comes from your portal account, not from a local setting.
</Note>

## Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are billed through Stripe. Use the customer portal link in your billing
email to update payment details, change plan, or cancel.
