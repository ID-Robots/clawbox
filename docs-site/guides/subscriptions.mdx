ClawBox hardware is yours to own. A **ClawBox AI subscription** is optional and powers the
managed AI experience, a few device features, a bigger backup allowance and a higher
support tier.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | ClawBox AI | ClawKeep backups | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | €0, free forever | — | Standard usage · portal access | 1 GB | Hardware, warranty, shipping, billing + **basic setup guidance** by email |
| **Pro** | €9 / month | €89 / year | 5× more usage than Free · Remote Desktop · priority processing | 5 GB | **Email support** for setup & configuration |
| **Max** | €49 / month (30-day trial) | €479 / year | Maximum usage · Remote Desktop · highest priority | 50 GB | **Full support — real humans via call/meeting** |

Yearly billing costs less than twelve monthly payments; the usage card on the box says how
much you save and links to the plan cards in your account.

<Note>
There is no model to pick in ClawBox AI chat on any plan. What the plan buys is how much
you get, plus the device features below. Your plan is shown on the ClawBox AI card in
**Settings → Providers** and comes from your account on clawbox.com, not from a local
setting. When the box cannot read its own settings for a moment, the plan shows as unknown
rather than as Free.
</Note>

| Feature | Free | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chat, and speech-to-text in the cloud | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| [Prime Radiant](/guides/prime-radiant) — adding documents (reading and reviewing work without it) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| [Memory Shard](/guides/memory-shard) | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Coding Agent | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Memory index in the ClawBox AI cloud | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Remote Desktop | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Spoken replies in the ClawBox AI cloud | — | — | ✓ |
| [Switching the box's edition](/editions/switching) | — | — | ✓ |

Where a feature needs a plan, its own first step says so and offers the upgrade — nothing
is hidden without an explanation. An upgrade reaches the box without a restart: cloud voices
appear as soon as the box reads the new plan.

<Note>
You can also run ClawBox with **your own** provider key or subscription, or with

</Note>

## Usage, allowances and credits

**Settings → Providers** shows a **ClawBox AI usage** card with what this box's account
has used:

- **Weekly chat allowance**, and when it frees up.
- **Short-term limit (5 hours)** — a shorter rolling window on top of the weekly one.
  Hitting it does not mean your week is gone.
- **This week** — meters for images, text-to-speech minutes, speech-to-text minutes and
  memory indexing.
- **Credits** — your balance, and what you have spent from it this week.

Credits are spent only after an allowance runs out, and paid plans can top them up with
**Top up**. When an allowance does run out, a failed chat message says which allowance is
spent and when it frees up — and, for the weekly allowance, how to add an extra-usage pack —
instead of a generic rate-limit error. See
[ClawBox AI → Usage and credits](/guides/clawbox-ai#usage-and-credits).

## Managing your subscription

Plans are managed in your account on [clawbox.com](https://clawbox.com/portal): the
**Upgrade** and **Open clawbox.com** buttons on the usage card take you there. Change plan,
switch between monthly and yearly billing, update payment details or cancel from there;
payments are handled by Stripe, and the customer portal link in your billing email works
too.
