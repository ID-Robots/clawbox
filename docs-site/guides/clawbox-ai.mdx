**ClawBox AI** is the managed AI service ClawBox ships with. Connect your box to your
ClawBox account and the assistant works immediately: no API key to obtain, no billing
relationship with a model vendor, nothing to keep running.

It is optional. You can run your box on [your own provider key or a
subscription](/setup/choose-ai-provider), or entirely on [the models on the
box](/guides/local-ai), and nothing on this page applies.

## Connecting your box

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the provider panel">
    **Settings → Providers**, or the **AI provider** step of the first-boot wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose ClawBox AI">
    It is the first row. Pick the plan you want on the card below it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the box">
    The box shows an **Open authorization page** link and a short code. Open the page —
    on your phone is fine — and enter the code there. The box waits and picks the
    authorization up by itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The token that results is stored on the device. Your plan is read from your account
afterwards, not set locally, so upgrading in your account is reflected on the box without
you re-entering anything.

<Note>
Changing which ClawBox account a box is linked to unpairs [ClawKeep](/guides/subscriptions)
backups with it — the backups belong to the account.
</Note>

## What each plan covers

The full price list is on [Subscriptions](/guides/subscriptions). What matters on the
device is this:

| Capability | Free | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chat | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Weekly chat allowance | standard | 5× Free | maximum |
| Speech to text in the cloud | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Spoken replies in the cloud | — | — | ✓ |
| Memory index in the cloud | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| [Memory Shard](/guides/memory-shard) and the Coding Agent | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| [Switching the box's edition](/editions/switching) | — | — | ✓ |
| Remote Desktop | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| ClawKeep cloud backups | 1 GB | 5 GB | 50 GB |

<Note>
On ClawBox AI there is no model to pick: the chat names the provider and shows no model
pill beside it. What a plan buys is how much you get and which device features are
unlocked. The reasoning control is there and starts at **Medium**.
</Note>

## What your plan powers

ClawBox AI is not only the chat model. On a connected box, three things that used to run
engines on the device default to the cloud your plan already covers:

| Capability | Where it runs by default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Speech to text** — your voice messages, turned into text | the cloud, on every connected box including Free | the transcription service is part of every plan |
| **Spoken replies** — the assistant reading an answer out loud | the cloud on **Max**; the voice on the box otherwise | the cloud voice is a Max feature, and pointing an unentitled box at it would only add a failed round trip |
| **Memory indexing** — turning your notes and documents into something searchable | the cloud on **Pro and Max**, on every edition, once the box has confirmed the service answers it | it needs a paid plan, and moving an index costs a full rebuild, so the box will not move it on a guess |

Two rules govern this, and both are deliberate:

- **It only ever moves work to the cloud, never back.** Nothing here quietly takes a
  capability off the cloud and puts it back on your box.
- **A choice you made yourself is never overwritten.** Every screen that lets you pick
  records that you picked. Pin a capability to the box and it stays pinned; choose the
  cloud, or hand it back to the default, and the box takes it from there.

You can see and change all three in **Settings → Local AI** and, for the memory index,
in [Memory Shard's settings](/guides/memory-shard#where-the-index-is-built).

When a capability is on the box rather than the cloud, the card says which of the reasons
applies — not connected, the plan does not cover it, the service did not answer, or you
picked the box yourself — so there is something to act on rather than a bare "not
available".

## Usage and credits

**Settings → Providers** shows a usage card for the connected account:

- **Weekly chat allowance** — how much of this week's allowance you have spent, and when
  it frees up.
- **5-hour burst** — a shorter rolling window on top of the weekly one. Hitting it does
  not mean your week is gone.
- **This week** — separate meters for images, text-to-speech minutes, speech-to-text
  minutes and memory indexing.
- **Credits** — your balance, and what you have spent from it this week. Credits are used
  only after an allowance runs out, and paid plans can top them up.

If an allowance runs out mid-conversation the chat says exactly which one, and when it
frees up, instead of failing silently.

<Note>
The numbers come from your ClawBox account. If the box cannot read them the card says so
and tries again shortly — it never invents a figure.
</Note>

## Changing or leaving

Switch to any other provider any time from **Settings → Providers**; the next message
uses it. Billing is handled in your ClawBox account — use the customer portal link in
your billing email to change plan or cancel.
