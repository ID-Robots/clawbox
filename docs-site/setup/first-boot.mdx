Everything happens in a browser, on the box's own Wi-Fi hotspot. No app to install, no code to scan,
no account to create first.

<Info>
**Hands-on time:** ~5 minutes · **Tools:** none · **Needed:** a phone or laptop with Wi-Fi, plus
Ethernet or Wi-Fi for the box itself. The wizard also runs a system update, which can take longer
and reboots the box.
</Info>

## What's in the box

- ClawBox unit (NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano 8 GB)
- 512 GB NVMe SSD with the OS pre-installed
- 20 W USB-C power adapter
- Ethernet cable

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Unbox and power on">
    Place the unit on a flat, ventilated surface, plug in Ethernet if you have it (recommended — it
    is the most reliable path), then connect the power adapter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the ClawBox-Setup hotspot">
    After about a minute the box broadcasts its own Wi-Fi network, **`ClawBox-Setup`** — open by
    default (it takes a password only if you set one later, in the wizard's security step). Join it from your phone or laptop. Its appearance in your Wi-Fi list is the signal that
    the box is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the wizard">
    Browse to **`http://10.42.0.1`**. If your home network already uses `10.42.0.0/24`, the box falls
    back to `http://10.43.0.1`.

    Some phones pop the page up on their own when they join the hotspot, but many will not — ClawBox
    does not hijack DNS to force it (see [Networking](/technical/networking#two-network-modes)), so
    typing the address is the reliable path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the five-step wizard">
    1. **Wi-Fi** — pick your network and enter its password. Ethernet is detected automatically. The
       box then leaves the hotspot to join your network, so you have to rejoin your own Wi-Fi before
       the wizard continues.
    2. **Update** — the box pulls the current release. This is not optional and can reboot the box.
    3. **Security** — set the device password. It is the same password for the browser login and for
       SSH. You can also rename the setup hotspot and give it a password here.
    4. **AI provider** — pick what powers the assistant, see [Choose your AI provider](/setup/choose-ai-provider). On a
       [Hermes](/editions/hermes) box this step shows the Hermes provider panel instead — see
       [AI Providers on Hermes](/editions/hermes-providers).
    5. **Telegram** *(optional)* — create a bot with [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) and paste
       its token. The wizard shows a QR code that opens BotFather on your phone. **Skip** is a
       first-class option; you can connect Telegram later from Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Setup finishes on the desktop. Open **Chat** and send your first message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
A reply to your first message means your ClawBox is live. 🦀
</Check>

## Reaching the device afterwards

Once the box has joined your network, the hotspot goes away and you reach it at:

1. `http://<device-ip>` — always works; find the IP in your router's client list (look for `clawbox`).
2. `http://clawbox.local` — mDNS, convenient but not universally reliable.

<Warning>
If `clawbox.local` doesn't load, **disable any active VPN** first (the most common cause), or use the
device's IP address. Some networks drop mDNS between Wi-Fi and wired segments entirely — Ethernet
plus a DHCP reservation is the most reliable setup. See
[Networking](/technical/networking#why-clawboxlocal-sometimes-fails--and-what-to-do).
</Warning>
