Get from a sealed box to a working AI assistant in a few minutes.

<Note>
  These steps are the same for both editions. Your box arrives already running the
  agent you chose at checkout — **OpenClaw** on ClawBox (Connect), or **Hermes Agent**
  on ClawBox Hermes Edition. There is nothing to install or select on first boot.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power on">
    Connect the included USB-C power supply and (optionally) an Ethernet cable.
    ClawBox boots in about a minute; the `ClawBox-Setup` network appearing in your Wi-Fi list is the signal that it is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the setup hotspot and open the wizard">
    On first boot the box has not joined your network yet — it hosts its own. From a phone
    or laptop, join the open **`ClawBox-Setup`** Wi-Fi network and browse to
    **`http://10.42.0.1`** (`http://10.43.0.1` if your network already uses `10.42.0.0/24`).
    See [First Boot](/setup/first-boot).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to the internet">
    Join your Wi-Fi or confirm the Ethernet connection, then rejoin your own network to
    continue. See [Connect to a Network](/setup/connect-network). Afterwards the box lives at
    `http://<device-ip>`, or `http://clawbox.local` where mDNS works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update and set your password">
    The wizard runs a system update, then asks you to set the device password — the same one
    for the browser login and for SSH.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your AI provider">
    ClawBox AI works out of the box. To use Claude, GPT, Gemini, or a local model,
    follow [Choose Your AI Provider](/setup/choose-ai-provider).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a chat app">
    Connect Telegram and message your ClawBox. You can also use the web chat from the ClawBox interface.
    See [Messaging Channels](/guides/messaging-channels).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Send a message like "Hello, what can you do?" from your linked chat app. A reply means your ClawBox is live.
</Check>
