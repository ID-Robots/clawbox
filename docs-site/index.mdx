## What ClawBox does

A small, always-on computer running a self-hosted agent that connects your chat apps to an AI. It's for people who want a private, dedicated AI assistant without renting cloud infrastructure or babysitting a server install.

### Two editions, same hardware

You choose which agent your box runs when you order. Both editions are the same NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super hardware at the same price.

| Edition | Runs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ClawBox (Connect)** | **OpenClaw** | Optional ClawBox AI plans available |
| **ClawBox Hermes Edition** | **Hermes Agent** by Nous Research | No ClawBox AI subscription required |

These are separate products at checkout, so your order is identifiable as one or the other through fulfilment, invoicing and support. The edition is set when the device is produced; on a box you already own, the **Max** plan can [switch it to the other edition](/editions/switching) from Settings. Most of this documentation applies to both editions; pages that are specific to one say so. See [Editions](/editions/overview) for the full comparison.

### Why people choose it

- **Owned hardware** — it lives on your desk, drawing as little as 7–15 W.
- **Multi-channel** — Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp, email and the built-in web chat all set up from Settings; advanced users can add more OpenClaw channels where supported.
- **Flexible AI** — ClawBox AI out of the box, a Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini subscription you already pay for, your own API key, or models that run on the box.
- **Your own documents** — [Memory Shard](/guides/memory-shard) indexes your notes and files so the assistant can actually find things in them.
- **Pre-configured** — no Linux setup, no Docker wrangling. Plug in and go.
